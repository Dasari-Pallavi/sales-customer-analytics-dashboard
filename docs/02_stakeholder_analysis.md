# Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest / Need | Influence | Interest Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales Head | Decision maker | Revenue by country, month and product | High | High |
| Marketing Manager | Primary user | Customer segments for campaigns | Medium | High |
| Finance Manager | Primary user | Revenue loss from cancellations | High | Medium |
| Operations Manager | User | Forecast for inventory planning | Medium | High |
| IT / Data Team | Support | Data access and refresh | Medium | Medium |
| Business Analyst (me) | Author | Requirements, analysis, delivery | Medium | High |

## RACI (for key deliverables)

| Deliverable | Sales Head | Marketing | Finance | Operations | IT | BA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BRD | A | C | C | C | I | R |
| KPI definitions | A | C | R | C | I | R |
| Dashboard | A | C | C | C | R | R |
| UAT sign-off | A | R | R | R | I | C |

R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed

## Engagement Plan
- Requirement workshop (1 hr) with each stakeholder.
- Sprint review at the end of each sprint.
- UAT session before sign-off.
