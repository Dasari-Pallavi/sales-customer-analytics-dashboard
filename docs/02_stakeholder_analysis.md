# 2. Stakeholder Analysis & RACI Matrix

| Stakeholder Role | Department | Key Business Questions | RACI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial Director** | Executive | What is our net revenue after cancellations, and how is it trending month-over-month? | **A** (Accountable) |
| **Business Analyst (You)** | IT / Analytics | Elicits requirements, defines KPIs/data rules, manages Jira backlog, leads UAT sign-off. | **R** (Responsible) |
| **CRM & Marketing Lead** | Marketing | Which customers are VIP Champions, and which high-value buyers are At-Risk of churning? | **C** (Consulted) |
| **Supply Chain Manager** | Operations | What will revenue look like over the next 6 months so we can plan Q4 stock? | **C** (Consulted) |
| **Regional Sales Leads** | Sales | How do international markets (Germany, France, EIRE) compare against UK domestic sales? | **I** (Informed) |
