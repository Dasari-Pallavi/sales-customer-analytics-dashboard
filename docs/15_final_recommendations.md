## Decision Support: What to Do First
1. Approve the At Risk win-back campaign (fast, low cost, measurable).
2. Plan peak-season stock using the forecast range.
3. Investigate cancellation spikes before changing policy.

## What This Analysis Cannot Tell You
- No cost data, so no profit conclusions.
- Missing customer IDs (about 23% of rows) mean segment results cover only identified customers.
- Two years of data give a limited view of seasonality; the forecast is a guide.
- Reasons for cancellations are not in the data.
